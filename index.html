About me

Hello, I'm Cherry

I believe we can all find some experiences in life difficult, and that taking the first steps towards counselling or therapy can be tough. I will help make the process less daunting and work with you through whatever you may be experiencing.

I'm a psychotherapist and counsellor who works in a person-centred way, offering and holding a safe, non-judgemental space for you to explore what is going on in your world. I offer individual or group counselling, and I can see you weekly or fortnightly.

I have specialised in working with people living with long-term and life-limiting illness, those caring for their loved ones, and those struggling to come to terms with bereavement. I have also facilitated bereavement groups for several years.

FAQ

Questions you might have

If your question isn't answered here, please get in touch — I'm happy to help.

What happens in the free initial session?

It's a relaxed, 30-minute conversation with no charge. It gives us the opportunity to discuss what you feel you need from your therapy and for us to see if we could work together. There's no obligation to continue afterwards.

How long are sessions and how often?

Each session lasts 50 minutes, and I see clients either weekly or fortnightly. Counselling is open-ended — we review how things are going together from time to time.

How much does counselling cost?

The initial 30-minute session is free. After that, I charge £60 for each 50-minute session. A concessionary rate can be negotiated if you are unwaged (students or unemployed).

Is what I say confidential?

Yes. What you share in our sessions is kept confidential. There are a few limits: if I believe there is a risk of harm to you or to someone else, or where I have a legal obligation to share information.

I have monthly supervision with a qualified supervisor, where I discuss my work without using your name. If I ever needed to break confidentiality, I would always try to speak to you first, unless safeguarding prevents it.

Can I have counselling online or by phone?

Yes. As well as face-to-face sessions in private, confidential rooms, I offer telephone counselling and online sessions via Zoom. Walking therapy is also available.

What happens to my personal information?

Your records are kept securely. They are held for three years after our counselling ends and are then securely destroyed. If you make an enquiry but decide not to go ahead, your details are deleted within one month.

You can read the full details in my privacy notice and GDPR policy (PDF).

Do you work with groups?

Yes. I offer individual or group counselling, and I have facilitated bereavement groups for several years.
